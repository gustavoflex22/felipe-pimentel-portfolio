BIO


Stylist and fashion consultant based in São Paulo with 2 years of experience working at UMS458-LL, I have developed a sharp eye for urban aesthetics and contemporary Brazilian street culture. My work crosses the territories of music, visual communication, and fashion, building a language grounded in authenticity and creative rigor.

Currently in my 4th semester of Fashion Business at Universidade Anhembi Morumbi, I bring together academic formation and practical industry experience, collaborating with artists, brands, and fashion productions.

My styling practice is marked by an attentive look at subcultural references, the relationship between clothing and identity, and the articulation between concept and execution — from set preparation to final image.
